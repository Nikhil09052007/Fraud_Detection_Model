# What is a Merchant?

A merchant is any business that accepts payments from customers in exchange for goods or services. Merchants connect to the payment ecosystem via a payment gateway and acquiring bank.

## Key Points

- Merchants have a category code (MCC) describing their business type
- Merchant identity and history are strong fraud signals (new merchant, high-risk category, etc.)
- Merchants can themselves be victims of fraud (chargebacks) or occasionally complicit in it
