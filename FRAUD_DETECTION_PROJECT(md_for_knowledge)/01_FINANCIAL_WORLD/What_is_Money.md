# What is Money?

Money is a socially and legally accepted medium of exchange — a way to trade value without bartering goods directly. In fraud systems, money almost never moves as physical cash; it moves as numbers changing in ledgers across banks.

## Key Points

- Functions of money: medium of exchange, store of value, unit of account
- In digital finance, money is mostly *represented* — a balance in a database, not a physical object
- Fraud is ultimately about someone moving or claiming this represented value illegitimately

## Why It Matters

Every fraud detection system exists to protect the integrity of these numeric representations of value.
