# Fraud Label

The target variable — a 0/1 flag on each transaction or account indicating whether it was fraudulent, usually derived from confirmed chargebacks, customer disputes, or investigator confirmation.

## Key Points

- Often delayed — the true label may not be known for days or weeks
- Noisy — proxies like chargebacks don't perfectly equal fraud (see Friendly Fraud)
- Label quality is often the single biggest constraint on model quality
