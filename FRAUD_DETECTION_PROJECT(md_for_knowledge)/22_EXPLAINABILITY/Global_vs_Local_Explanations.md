# Global vs Local Explanations

Global explanations describe what drives the model's behavior overall, across the whole population; local explanations describe why one specific transaction received its specific score.

## Key Points

- Global: 'transaction velocity is generally the top driver of fraud risk across the model'
- Local: 'this specific transaction was flagged because of unusually high velocity and a new device'
- Analysts investigating a single flagged transaction need local explanations
