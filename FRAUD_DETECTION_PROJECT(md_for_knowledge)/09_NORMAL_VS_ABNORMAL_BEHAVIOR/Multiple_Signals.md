# Multiple Signals

The principle that no single abnormal signal alone is usually enough — fraud risk emerges from the combination and co-occurrence of multiple deviations.

## Key Points

- Models learn to weigh and combine many weak signals into one strong prediction
- This is precisely why ML tends to outperform simple single-rule systems for this problem

## Why It Matters

This idea is the conceptual justification for using ML (which can combine many weak/noisy features) over simple hand-written rules.
