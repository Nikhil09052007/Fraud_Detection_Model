# Probability vs Risk

A model's output probability is a statistical estimate of fraud likelihood; 'risk' additionally incorporates the potential impact (e.g. transaction amount, customer value) — two transactions with equal fraud probability can carry very different risk.

## Key Points

- Risk = likelihood × impact, not likelihood alone
- A $10,000 transaction at 5% fraud probability may warrant more caution than a $10 transaction at 20% fraud probability
