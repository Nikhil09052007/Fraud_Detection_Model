# Fraudulent vs Legitimate Transaction

A legitimate transaction is authorized by the true account owner; a fraudulent one is not — regardless of how 'normal' it might look on the surface.

## Key Points

- This distinction is the ground-truth label your model is trained to predict
- Not always cleanly knowable at the time of the transaction — often confirmed later via customer dispute
- Ambiguous or unconfirmed cases require careful labeling policy
