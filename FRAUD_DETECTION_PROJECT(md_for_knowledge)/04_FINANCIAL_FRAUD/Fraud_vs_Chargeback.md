# Fraud vs Chargeback

A chargeback is a formal reversal of a transaction, initiated by a cardholder or issuer. It's a *signal* often used as a proxy for fraud, but not every chargeback is fraud (some are legitimate disputes or 'friendly fraud').

## Key Points

- Chargebacks are frequently used as the fraud label in datasets — but they're a noisy proxy
- Friendly fraud (a real charge disputed dishonestly) still produces a chargeback but isn't 'true' fraud in the classic sense
- Understanding this nuance matters when defining your target variable
