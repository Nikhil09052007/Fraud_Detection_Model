# Why Fraud is Hard to Detect

Fraud is rare, adversarial, constantly evolving, and often only confirmed long after the transaction — making it one of the hardest real-world ML problems.

## Key Points

- Extreme class imbalance — fraud is often <1% of transactions
- Adversarial: fraudsters actively adapt to evade detection
- Delayed and noisy labels — 'fraud' is often confirmed weeks later via chargeback
- High cost asymmetry — missing fraud and falsely blocking legitimate users are both costly, but differently
