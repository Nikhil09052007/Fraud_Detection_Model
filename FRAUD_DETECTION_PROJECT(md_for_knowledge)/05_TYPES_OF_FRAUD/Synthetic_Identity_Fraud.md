# Synthetic Identity Fraud

Creating a new, fake identity by combining real (often stolen) and fabricated information — e.g. a real SSN with a fake name and date of birth — to build credit history and eventually 'bust out'.

## Key Points

- Doesn't map to any single real victim, making it hard to detect via customer complaints
- Often built up slowly over months to establish credibility
- One of the hardest fraud types to catch because it looks like a 'thin file' legitimate customer
