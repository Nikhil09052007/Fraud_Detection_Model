# Interview Mental Model

A structured way to answer 'design a fraud detection system' in an interview: (1) clarify the business goal and constraints, (2) define the ML problem precisely, (3) discuss data and features, being explicit about leakage, (4) discuss model choice and imbalance handling, (5) discuss evaluation metrics and thresholds, (6) discuss the real-time system and decisioning, (7) discuss monitoring and retraining.

## Key Points

- Mirrors the exact structure of this repo, in order
- Interviewers are usually more impressed by structured tradeoff reasoning than by naming a specific algorithm
- Always close by tying back to business impact — fraud loss, false declines, and ROI
